# Background — dontforget

## Why This Exists

No general-purpose daily calendar of tragic history exists. Everything is either all-scope archives (Internet Archive), single-event memorials (9/11 Museum), commercial obituary platforms (Legacy.com), or statistics-only databases (Mapping Police Violence). Nobody shows you *what happened on this day* across all categories of loss.

## Key Gaps This Fills

1. **The near past is a black hole.** Newspaper archives cut off at 1963 (copyright). TV archives start at 2009. The 1960s–2000s and recently-receded stories fall through every crack.
2. **Individual memory and structural context never meet.** Obituary platforms preserve the person but not the why. Justice databases preserve statistics but not the person. This site puts events and lives lost side by side on the same date.
3. **Global South tragedies are structurally under-remembered.** Western, English-first archives dominate. The worst-suffering places are the worst-documented. Conscious effort needed to close this gap.
