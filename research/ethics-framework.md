# Content Policy — dontforget

## Sourcing Standards

- **Two independent sources** per memory event (reputable news outlets, official records)
- **Verifiable public source** per promise (video, transcript, news article with exact quote)
- Prefer primary sources. Where accounts conflict, state the conflict.
- **Never scrape.** Every entry written from verified sources in our own words.

## Language Rules

- "Died by suicide" — never "committed suicide" or "suicide victim"
- Avoid method details. Never include suicide notes. Avoid speculation on cause.
- State causes of death factually and neutrally. Describe the person, not the stereotype.
- Always include help resources on suicide-related content.

## Content Boundaries

- **No gore, death images, or last-moment recordings**
- **No engagement bait:** no "most viewed," no rankings, no auto-play, no sad-music autoplay
- **No ads on memory pages**
- **Factual and restrained.** What happened, how many lost, where to read more. No commentary.

## Corrections

- Corrections are a git commit. Visible correction note on the page.
- If a living person disputes a promise entry, review and correct promptly.

## Copyright

- **Never republish photographs** without license or permission
- **Write original summaries** in your own words (facts only)
- **Quote at most 2–3 sentences** with attribution and link
- Link out rather than reproduce full articles

## Defamation

- The dead are beyond defamation in most jurisdictions. Living people in promise entries (politicians, public figures) are not.
- Never publish allegations of criminality as fact without conviction or court record.
- Promises must be sourced to the person's own public statement.

## Inclusivity

- Include non-Western events. The worst-suffering places are the worst-documented.
- When adding events, consciously look beyond US/European news coverage.
